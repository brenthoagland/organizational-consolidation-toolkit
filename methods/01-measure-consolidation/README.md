# 01 · An Initial Assessment of Organizational Consolidation

Measure how tightly ascriptive statuses align with local criteria. The script measures each pair with the statistic matched to its levels of measurement (phi, point-biserial, eta, Cramér's V, as in the article's Figure 4), reports it as a 0–1 magnitude, and adds mutual information. Read the later sections against this diagnostic; [`docs/interpreting-results.md`](../../docs/interpreting-results.md) says how.

- **Run on your own data:** `measure-consolidation.R` (calls functions in the repo-root `R_functions/`).
